📂 README: The One Inefficiency No One Talks About in Indian Startups – Knowledge Mismanagement

📌 Overview:
- This presentation explores an often-ignored but high-impact operational inefficiency plaguing Indian startups: poor knowledge management (KM).
- Backed by research from McKinsey, NASSCOM, and real startup case studies, it unpacks how gaps in documenting decisions, processes, and tribal knowledge quietly drain billions in productivity, trust, and innovation.

🔍 Strategic Context:
Too often, startup playbooks glamorize hustle culture while treating documentation as overhead. This deck reframes KM as a strategic advantage and presents actionable remedies proven to enhance scaling capacity, employee experience, and investor trust.

💡 Author's Note:
This deck is designed to provoke introspection among founders, product leads, and ops teams about the cost of ignoring KM. The goal is to shift conversations from short-term velocity to long-term sustainability.
